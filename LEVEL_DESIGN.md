# Level design

Five connected areas contain 26 rooms in total. Each room is 26 world units wide. The visual scale makes furniture large relative to the ghost. There is a checkpoint at each room boundary and four optional memories in each area.

| Area | Sequence | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Attic | Awakening, first lamp, shadows, moving rafter, attic exit | Learn movement, jump, possess, reveal and leave an object |
| ChildRoom | Car tunnel, lamp/car route, cupboard, first music bridge, melody crossing | Use a low vehicle route and time a spectral bridge |
| Kitchen | First updraft, blown crate, sink lights, steam valve, resonance crossing | Use persistent wind after leaving the fan; move light objects |
| Workshop | Moving shelves, gear timing, heavy latch, clock/heavy gate, music exit | Control local time and use weight |
| ClockHall | Light, air, local time, weight, music, car spindle/ascent | Combine the learned spatial mechanics in a final sequence |

The final car releases the clock's inner latch; the ghost then climbs the clock shelves. Memories use optional upper routes or illumination/music requirements. An ending revisitation menu supports collecting missed memories without deleting the completed run.

Keyboard and touch share the same mechanics. Hold R or use pause/reset to restore only the current room. No puzzle needs advertising. Optional rewarded ads reveal a contextual hint. Interstitials are eligible only between areas, after the initial grace period and cooldown.

First-play target: 30–45 minutes. This is a design target, not a measured duration. Automated traversal and a fresh human playtest are separate checks; final playtime should be recorded after a complete unfamiliar-player run.
